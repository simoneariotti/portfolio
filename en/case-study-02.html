Askme AI: from conversation to action

The suite's native agent, designed from scratch. Not a chatbot that answers: a system of components that makes AI operational inside the processes, next to the user.

01 · Context

An AI alongside, not an AI apart.

The vision was to bring artificial intelligence inside the modules of Askme: an agent always alongside and in context, speeding up the operations users have to perform on the objects in the suite anyway. Before, there was no agent: there were AI features, but no operational agent working next to people.

  • The AgentThat is what it is called inside the suite: built to live inside the modules, not beside them.
  • My partI designed it from scratch: no rework, virgin ground, the freedom to give shape to a new paradigm while keeping the posts of the real constraints firmly in the ground. It is the part of my work I am proudest of.
  • From scratch, not aloneThe paradigm is mine, the limits came from a constant back and forth with the people who build it. The two corrected each other throughout the project.

02 · The problem

Natural language opens the dialogue, then the action happens somewhere else.

Natural language is perfect for opening a dialogue with an AI. An agent that only answers, though, leaves the user to finish the action somewhere else.

  • Where it breaksAs soon as the operation gets complicated, free text loses pieces: ambiguous information, missing data, no guarantee about what happens next.
  • The jump to makeFrom an AI that talks to an AI that operates. Not a chatbot stuck onto the product, but an agent that gathers the information and performs the actions inside the suite, without taking the user out of the flow.

03 · The reasoning

Natural language to talk, components to operate.

The founding decision of the whole agent is a single one: free text is fine for conversation, but when an operation gets complex you need structure. At that point the AI stops chatting and gathers or presents information to precise usability standards, which is to say components. It is the same principle as a good interviewer: when context matters, you ask specific questions instead of letting someone ramble, so nothing gets lost.

Everything else follows from that choice. Three ways it takes shape.

  • The answer becomes an actionable object. The agent does not return text to read: it returns a card with the AI-generated contextual summary and, beside it, the exact action to take. Conversation becomes work without changing screen.
  • Complex operations move into structured flows. When several steps or several pieces of data are needed, the agent opens a guided flow inside the conversation, with explicit inputs instead of free text: no information lost, no ambiguity about what happens next.
  • All in the product's own language. Every surface the AI generates uses the same Askme components, so it feels like part of the product and not a foreign body. And every output carries a validation control: the AI proposes, the person confirms.

Every AI output is a draft to validate, not a final answer. The interface exists precisely to make that validation easy.

04 · The shape of the decision

The structure, not the chat.

The diagrams are abstract: schemas and structures, not the real product interfaces.

05 · Outcome and principles

Less time to operate, AI inside the work.

The agent is designed and in progressive rollout, and the direction of the project is settled: the actions that today require opening screens, searching, filling in and confirming are to be brought back inside the flow of the conversation, on the objects the user is already looking at. For the user that means operational ease, with the knowledge base a request away, inside a system that speaks the language of the product they already use. Each iteration refines its behaviour, voice and limits.

What is missing is the measurement. An agent that operates is judged on one thing only: how many steps it removes from an operation the user would perform anyway. That is the number I want, and I do not have it yet, because the agent is too young for a comparison to mean anything. Saying it is faster without having counted would be exactly what I refuse to do on the design system.

It is exactly the kind of problem, giving shape to a paradigm while building it, where design counts for most.

A principle that emerged

AI design is retracing the history of the web: first text, then inputs, then components. Conversation is the starting point, not the finish line.
